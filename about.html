Our Story

Al-Estekama School was founded with a simple but powerful mission: to give every student the best possible start in life through quality education and strong values.

The name "الاستقامة" means integrity and steadfastness — qualities that sit at the heart of everything we do every single day.

We have grown into a vibrant school community, proud of our students' achievements and committed to their futures.
